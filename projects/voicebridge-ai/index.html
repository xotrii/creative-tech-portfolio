    Architecture, privacy, and limitations

    The interface separates speech recognition, translation, and speech synthesis into distinct states. The included provider supports selected phrases and user-defined glossary substitutions only. Unknown language is not presented as a real AI translation. A production version would connect a reviewed translation provider through a server-side adapter, then add authentication, consent, logging controls, latency monitoring, and telephony infrastructure.
