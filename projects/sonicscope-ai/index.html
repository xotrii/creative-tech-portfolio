Algorithms and limitations

Peak, RMS, crest factor, and zero-crossing rate are deterministic measurements. BPM is estimated from short-time energy peaks. Key uses autocorrelation to identify a dominant periodic pitch and is not reliable for dense or percussive mixes. Visual frequency bins use a small DFT window for an educational overview, not mastering-grade analysis.
